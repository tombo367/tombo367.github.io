Tom Borrett

PhD student · Cavendish Laboratory, University of Cambridge

I work on cosmology from galaxy clusters and the cosmic microwave background. Right now that means using machine learning to confirm galaxy clusters detected via the thermal Sunyaev-Zel'dovich effect, and building agentic AI systems, like CMBAgent, that can plan, code, run and interpret pieces of cosmological analysis themselves. I'm supervised by Boris Bolliet and affiliated with the Kavli Institute for Cosmology and the Infosys-Cambridge AI Centre. Before this I did an MPhil in Data Intensive Science at Cambridge, on parameter inference with diffusion models.

Projects

  • Galaxy clusters Confirming ACT clusters and finding their redshifts from the red sequence. Try finding one yourself.
  • Early dark energy How accurate do emulators need to be to test a fix for the Hubble tension? With a toy universe to fit by hand, and an AI agent on the night shift.
